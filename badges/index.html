Every Badge Tells a Weather Story

From glorious sunshine to spectacularly bad luck.

Worth the trip

🚗

Worth the Drive

Best weather gain per km

When it appears A place at least ~30 km away that is clearly better than where you are — warmer in warm mode, or cooler in cool mode — with enough weather gain for the drive. Skipped when a Weather Curse or a severe forecast drop would spoil the trip. Road-isolated island/mainland mismatches are excluded.

💰

Budget Pick

Good weather, closer distance — saves fuel

When it appears The most efficient nearby escape: solid temperature gain for fewer kilometres (at least ~30 km, usually within half your search radius). Ranked against other places in range — typically the top budget pick.

Sunshine & warmth

Warm & Dry

Max warmth with good conditions

When it appears Warm enough for the season (≥28 °C in Jun–Aug, ≥22 °C in May/Sep, ≥20 °C otherwise), not rainy or snowy today, wind ≤30 km/h, little precipitation, and mostly dry across the next few forecast days. Not shown with Heatwave or Beach Paradise.

🌊

Beach Paradise

Perfect beach weather — warm, sunny, light wind

When it appears Only on beaches: 20–35 °C, sunny or partly cloudy, wind ≤25 km/h.

☀️

Sunny Streak

Days of sunshine in a row — stable weather

When it appears The selected day is sunny, at least 3 of the next 5 forecast slots are sunny, temperature ≥10 °C, and there’s a long sunny day (≥8 hours of sunshine when that data is available). Not shown with Heatwave.

Heatwave

Several scorching days with no heavy rain

When it appears At least 3 days at or above 34 °C, and no heavy rain or thunderstorm in that window. In cool-search mode it only shows if the place is still clearly cooler than where you started.

🐇

Spring Awakening

Pure spring: mild, sunny & perfect for exploring

When it appears Only 1 March–15 May, north of about 45°N: 16–24 °C, sunny, wind ≤20 km/h.

Indian Summer

Autumn at its golden best: mild, sunny and perfect for exploring

When it appears You’re starting from roughly 40°N or further north. Destinations north of 45°N: 1 Sep–31 Oct. Between 35°N and 45°N: 1 Oct–30 Nov. Selected day sunny, 15–29 °C, wind ≤20 km/h, plus at least 2 matching days in a 5-day window. Deserts are excluded.

Plot twists

🌈

Weather Miracle

Bad today → sunny & warmer soon

When it appears Today is rainy, snowy or windy, and tomorrow or the day after turns sunny with at least 5 °C warmer.

⛈️

Weather Curse

Looking good now — deteriorating soon

When it appears Today is warmer than 10 °C with decent weather (sunny to overcast), then at least 2 of the next 3 days turn rainy, snowy or windy, and it cools by 5 °C or more.

Snow & rain

⛄

Snow King

Reliable snow — built for cold adventures

When it appears Places under ~150k population. Winter (Nov–Mar), or high Alps above 2000 m year-round. Qualifies via heavy snow (≥20 mm/24h, avg ≤2 °C), consistent snow (≥3 snow days, ≥10 mm, avg ≤0 °C), or deep freeze (≥1 snow day, avg ≤−5 °C, max ≤−1 °C).

🌧️

Rainy Days

A proper wet spell — not just a drizzle

When it appears At least 3 rainy days in the near forecast, including at least one heavy or intense rain.

Badge rules come straight from the SunNomad app. Thresholds can interact — for example Heatwave suppresses Warm & Dry and Sunny Streak, and Beach Paradise replaces Warm & Dry on beaches.
