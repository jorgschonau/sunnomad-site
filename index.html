How It Works

The World Looks Better in the Sun

From Corsica to Temple Bar

Weather planning for life on the road

SunNomad helps van lifers, campers, and road trippers find better weather — before they drive into the rain.

Find sunny destinations nearby

Open the map, set a search radius, and see where the weather looks good. SunNomad shows you sunny spots around your current location or anywhere in Europe — perfect when you're deciding where to head next.

Plan around the forecast

Pick a day and filter by weather conditions. Whether you're in a camper van, motorhome, or tent, SunNomad helps you avoid grey skies and find the sun — without juggling five different weather apps.

Discover places worth stopping for

Every destination comes with photos, context, and a clear weather read. From coastal roads to desert campsites, SunNomad turns weather data into trip inspiration — so you park where it feels good.

Built for nomads, not tourists

SunNomad is made for people who live on the road: van lifers chasing warmth, weekend campers escaping the city, and overlanders planning their next leg. Simple, fast, and focused on where you actually want to be.

About SunNomad

SunNomad is a weather and trip-planning app built for people who live on the road — van lifers, campers, motorhomers, and anyone who'd rather follow the sun than sit in the rain.

Always know where the weather is good. Set a radius, pick a day, and find your next sunny stop. No clutter, no guesswork — just a clear map of where life feels better under blue skies.

SunNomad is currently available as an iOS beta via TestFlight. The full App Store release is coming soon. Available in English and German, with French on the way.

Frequently asked questions

What is SunNomad?

SunNomad is a weather app for van life, camping, and road trips. It shows you sunny destinations on a map so you can plan where to go based on the forecast — not the other way around.

Who is SunNomad for?

Van lifers, campers, motorhomers, overlanders, and anyone who travels by road and cares about the weather. If you've ever changed plans because of rain, SunNomad is for you.

How do I get the app?

Join the iOS beta via TestFlight using the button at the top of this page. The App Store release is coming soon — sign up for updates if you'd like to be notified.

Does SunNomad work in Europe?

Yes. SunNomad covers destinations across Europe and helps you find sunny spots wherever your road trip takes you.

Is SunNomad free?

The beta is free to join via TestFlight. Pricing for the full release will be announced when the app launches on the App Store.
