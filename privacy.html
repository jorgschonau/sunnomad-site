Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SunNomad is built by GoldieApps. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the app, where it is stored, and what you can do about it.

1. Account & profile data

SunNomad requires an account. When you register, we store:

  • Email address and password (managed by Supabase Auth — we never store your password in plain text)
  • Username (required) and optional display name
  • Account metadata such as when your account was created and when you last opened the app

This data is stored on our servers (Supabase) so you can sign in and sync favourites across devices.

2. Favourites & feedback

  • Favourites — saved places are stored in your account on our servers, linked to your user ID. They sync when you log in on another device.
  • Feedback — if you send feedback through the app, we store your message and may include your email address if you provide it.

3. Data stored on your device only

The following stays on your phone and is not uploaded to our servers:

  • App settings (theme, units, language, map radius, filters)
  • Onboarding progress and UI preferences
  • Cached map data for faster loading
  • Your login session token

4. Location

SunNomad uses your location while the app is open to centre the map and show nearby destinations. We do not store your GPS coordinates on our servers.

Coordinates may be sent to third-party services when you use certain features:

  • Open-Meteo — to fetch weather forecasts for a location
  • Google Places — when you search for a place (search text, not continuous tracking)
  • Apple / Google Maps — when you tap to navigate to a destination

5. Analytics & crash reporting

In production builds, we use:

  • Mixpanel (EU servers) — anonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g. screens viewed, features used, search queries). After login, events are linked to your user ID, not your email.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports to help us fix bugs.

We do not use advertising trackers and do not sell your data.

6. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather data
  • Google Places & Maps — place search and navigation
  • Mixpanel — product analytics (EU)
  • Sentry — crash reporting

These providers may process data on servers outside the EU. We choose services with appropriate safeguards where available.

7. Data retention

Account data, profile, and favourites are kept until you delete your account. Weather forecast data on our servers is automatically purged after a few weeks and is not linked to your identity.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Delete your account — in the app under Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, and favourites from our servers.
  • Request access or correction — email us at hola@sunnomad.app
  • Withdraw consent — you can stop using the app and delete your account at any time

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have additional rights under GDPR including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's privacy

SunNomad is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
