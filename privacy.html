2. Overview

SunNomad is a mobile app that helps you discover sunny travel destinations based on weather data. This policy explains what data we process, why, and what rights you have.

Summary: Core map and weather browsing works without an account. Optional account features (favourites sync, profile) store account data on our EU Supabase backend. We use Mixpanel (EU) for product analytics and Sentry for crash reporting. Device location is used only while you use the app and is not stored on our servers. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

3. Data We Process

3.1 Device Location

With your permission, the app accesses your device location while using the app (foreground only). Location is used to:

  • center the map and show destinations near you,
  • calculate distances,
  • personalise weather comparisons relative to your position.

Location is processed on your device for these features and is not stored as your user location on our servers. You can revoke access in system settings.

3.2 Optional Account & Profile (Supabase, EU)

If you create an account, we store:

  • email address and authentication credentials (handled by Supabase Auth),
  • profile fields such as username / display name and app preferences (e.g. language, units, search radius, theme),
  • favourites you save (synced to our database so they are available across devices),
  • optional in-app feedback you submit,
  • basic usage counters needed for in-app features (e.g. app open count for profile copy).

Guest use of the map and destination content does not require an account. Destination and weather content itself is public reference data, not personal data about you.

3.3 Product Analytics (Mixpanel EU)

We use Mixpanel with an EU data residency endpoint to understand how the app is used (e.g. screens opened, feature taps). Events are tied to an anonymous device id; after login they may be associated with your account id so we can improve the product. We do not use Mixpanel for advertising or selling data.

3.4 Crash & Error Reporting (Sentry)

We use Sentry to collect crash reports and technical diagnostics (device/app version, stack traces). This helps us fix bugs. Reports are not used for advertising.

3.5 Maps & Weather Third Parties

  • Google Maps — map display and related map services (subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy).
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts; requests use geographic coordinates for the destination or area you are viewing.

3.6 What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising SDKs or ad identifiers for ads
  • No sale of personal data
  • No cookies or tracking scripts on this marketing website

4. Legal Basis (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Art. 6(1)(a) — Consent: device location permission.
  • Art. 6(1)(b) — Contract: creating and operating your optional account, favourites sync, and related account features.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests: operating the app (serving destination/weather data), product analytics, and crash reporting to keep the service reliable and improve it. You may object where applicable.

5. Retention

Account, profile, favourites, and feedback data are kept while your account exists. Device location is not retained on our servers as a stored “home location”. Analytics and crash data are retained according to the respective provider settings and our need to debug/improve the app. After account deletion, personal account data is removed from our systems as described below.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties / Processors

We do not sell personal data. We use processors/services to run the app:

  • Supabase (EU) — auth, database, account features
  • Mixpanel (EU endpoint) — product analytics
  • Sentry — crash/error reporting
  • Google Maps — maps
  • Open-Meteo — weather data

Some providers may process data outside the EEA under appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses), depending on their infrastructure. Mixpanel analytics for SunNomad are configured to use the EU API endpoint.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under the GDPR you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). This removes your account and associated personal data from our backend (including favourites tied to that account), subject to short technical retention needed to complete deletion and any legal obligations. Contact: hola@sunnomad.app.

8. Children

SunNomad is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The current version is always at sunnomad.app/privacy.

10. This Website

This static marketing site does not use cookies, local storage, or tracking scripts.

2. Überblick

SunNomad ist eine mobile App, mit der du sonnige Reiseziele anhand von Wetterdaten entdecken kannst. Diese Erklärung beschreibt, welche Daten wir verarbeiten, wozu, und welche Rechte du hast.

Kurzfassung: Karte und Wetter funktionieren ohne Konto. Optionale Account-Features (Favourites-Sync, Profil) speichern Kontodaten auf unserem EU-Supabase-Backend. Wir nutzen Mixpanel (EU) für Produkt-Analytics und Sentry für Crash-Reports. Der Gerätestandort wird nur bei Nutzung der App verwendet und nicht als Nutzerstandort auf unseren Servern gespeichert. Konto löschen geht jederzeit in der App.

3. Verarbeitete Daten

3.1 Gerätestandort

Mit deiner Erlaubnis greift die App auf den Standort zu („Bei Nutzung der App“ / Vordergrund). Verwendung:

  • Karte zentrieren und Ziele in der Nähe zeigen,
  • Entfernungen berechnen,
  • Wettervergleiche relativ zu deiner Position.

Der Standort wird dafür auf dem Gerät verarbeitet und nicht als dein Nutzerstandort auf unseren Servern gespeichert. Widerruf jederzeit in den Systemeinstellungen.

3.2 Optionales Konto & Profil (Supabase, EU)

Wenn du ein Konto anlegst, speichern wir:

  • E-Mail und Auth-Daten (über Supabase Auth),
  • Profilfelder wie Username / Anzeigename und App-Einstellungen (z. B. Sprache, Einheiten, Radius, Theme),
  • gespeicherte Favourites (Sync in unserer Datenbank),
  • optionales In-App-Feedback,
  • einfache Nutzungszähler für App-Features (z. B. App-Open-Count).

Die Nutzung von Karte und Zielinhalten ohne Konto ist möglich. Ziel- und Wetterinhalte sind öffentliche Referenzdaten, keine personenbezogenen Daten über dich.

3.3 Produkt-Analytics (Mixpanel EU)

Wir nutzen Mixpanel mit EU-Datenresidenz, um die App-Nutzung zu verstehen (z. B. Screens, Feature-Taps). Events hängen an einer anonymen Geräte-ID; nach Login können sie mit deiner Account-ID verknüpft werden. Keine Nutzung für Werbung oder Datenverkauf.

3.4 Crash- & Fehlerberichte (Sentry)

Wir nutzen Sentry für Crash-Reports und technische Diagnosen (Gerät/App-Version, Stack Traces), um Bugs zu beheben — nicht für Werbung.

3.5 Karten & Wetter

  • Google Maps — Kartendarstellung (eigene Bedingungen/Datenschutz von Google).
  • Open-Meteo — Wetterprognosen; Anfragen mit Koordinaten des betrachteten Orts/Gebiets.

3.6 Was wir nicht tun

  • Keine Werbe-SDKs / keine Ad-IDs für Ads
  • Kein Verkauf personenbezogener Daten
  • Keine Cookies/Tracking-Skripte auf dieser Marketing-Website
