Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SunNomad ("we", "our", or "the app") is built by GoldieApps. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

SunNomad collects only what's necessary to function:

  • Location data (GPS) — used only on-device to show nearby destinations. Never stored on our servers.
  • Favorites — stored locally on your device only.
  • Anonymous usage data — via Mixpanel analytics. No personal data attached.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Location is used solely to center the map and find nearby destinations.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not store your location on our servers.
  • Analytics help us improve the app — all data is anonymous.

3. Third Party Services

SunNomad uses the following third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather data
  • Google Maps — map display and place search
  • Supabase — destination database (no personal data stored)
  • Mixpanel — anonymous usage analytics

4. Data Retention

Location data is never stored. Favorites are stored locally on your device and cleared when you delete the app.

5. Children's Privacy

SunNomad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing to delete or export. Questions? Contact us below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
